After performing this experiment, the learner will be able to:

- Understand the principle of the Rockwell hardness test.
- Determine the Rockwell hardness number (HR) of a metallic specimen using the appropriate Rockwell scale.
- Study the effect of major and minor loads on indentation depth.
- Become familiar with the construction and working of the Rockwell hardness testing machine.
- Interpret hardness values for comparing engineering materials and selecting suitable materials for practical applications.
